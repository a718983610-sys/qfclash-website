QFClash 隐私说明

版本：1.0 ｜ 生效日期：自 QFClash 1.0.0 发布之日起生效 ｜ 适用范围：QFClash 安卓版、QFClash Windows 版，以及与前述应用共用同一账号体系的官方网站与论坛服务

一、引言

本《隐私说明》说明 QFClash 在提供软件、账号及相关服务的过程中，处理哪些数据、为何处理、如何保存，以及你享有哪些权利、如何行使。

本说明是《QFClash 用户使用协议》第七条的展开与细化。本说明与《QFClash 用户使用协议》不一致的，以《QFClash 用户使用协议》为准。

二、运营者与联系方式

QFClash 由个人维护者运营。隐私相关咨询、账号数据查询与权利请求，请通过以下方式联系：

  • 电子邮箱：a718983610@gmail.com

处理涉及账号数据的请求前，维护者会先核实申请人身份，以防数据被他人冒领、误改或误删。

三、我们处理的数据

（一）仅保存在你的设备本地的数据

下列数据仅保存在你的设备本地（应用私有目录），维护者不主动收集、不上传、不出售：

  1. 订阅链接与订阅配置；
  2. 节点选择、流量统计、测速记录；
  3. 软件设置；
  4. 诊断日志（仅在你主动导出并发送时，维护者才会收到，并仅用于处理你反馈的问题）。

你可在软件内清空配置与统计。卸载后，应用私有目录中的数据通常会随卸载删除；你主动导出并保存在其他位置的文件可能继续存在，请在卸载前自行检查并删除不再需要的文件。

（二）使用账号服务时在服务器侧处理的数据

你注册、登录并使用账号功能时，维护者会在服务器侧处理下列数据：

  1. 账号资料：你自设的账户 ID、电子邮箱地址、经不可逆加密处理的密码、账号状态与注册时间。
  2. 个人资料：你设置的昵称与头像。昵称与头像会在本软件、论坛与好友场景中公开显示；账户 ID 仅向你的好友可见；电子邮箱地址不对外显示，仅你本人在「我的」中可见。
  3. 登录与设备信息：登录设备的设备名称、平台类型（安卓 / Windows）、最近活跃时间、登录地区，以及设备标识哈希（构成与用途见第四节）。
  4. 会话与安全记录：登录令牌的签发与使用记录、验证码的发送与核验记录（含查看兑换码时的核验记录）、登录与安全操作日志、封禁与申诉记录。
  5. 积分与兑换记录：计时心跳记录、积分流水（每笔增减的时间、事由与余额）、兑换订单与审核记录。兑换美元奖励时，还包括你填写的发放信息：USDT（TRC20）收款地址，或你选择礼品卡时填写的接收邮箱，以及发放状态与发放备注；该等信息仅用于审核与发放，仅你本人与维护者可见。
  6. 你主动提交的内容：意见反馈内容与你自愿填写的联系方式（提交时系统会自动附带应用版本、平台信息，登录状态下附带账号信息，以便定位问题）；你在论坛发布的帖子、回复，以及与之相关的审核、举报与处置记录。
  7. 好友与加密聊天数据：好友关系与好友申请记录；聊天的加密公钥包（身份公钥、签名预钥与一次性预钥）；聊天消息的密文；以及经你的账号密码派生密钥加密后的聊天密钥备份。聊天采用端到端加密：服务器只保存与中继密文及公钥材料，无法读取消息明文；加密备份的密钥材料经你设置的密码派生加密，维护者无法解密。
  8. 通行密钥公钥凭证：你创建通行密钥后，服务器保存其公钥凭证、凭证名称、创建时间、最近使用时间与签名计数。通行密钥的私钥始终保存在你的设备或系统通行密钥服务中，不会上传至服务器。
  9. 谷歌登录信息：你选择谷歌登录时，维护者在谷歌返回的信息范围内读取你的电子邮箱地址，用于创建或匹配账号。同一邮箱通过谷歌登录与通过邮箱注册的，视为同一账号。

使用访客模式无需注册账号。为核算访客每日可用额度，服务器会处理设备标识哈希及与额度核算有关的日期、已用时长记录。

（三）联网时第三方可能处理的连接信息

  1. 检查更新：你主动检查更新时，软件会向官方仓库或相关发布服务发送请求，该等服务提供方可能按其适用的隐私规则处理你的 IP 地址、请求时间、客户端版本等连接信息。
  2. 导入或更新订阅：软件会向你指定的订阅地址发送请求，相应服务商及网络服务提供方可能获取该请求所需的连接信息，其处理行为由其自身规则约束，维护者无法控制。
  3. 账号请求：登录、注册与验证请求由软件直接向账号服务器发出，不经代理节点转发。
  4. 邮件发送：注册、找回密码、更换邮箱、异常验证、兑换发放通知等邮件，经邮件发送服务发出，该服务提供方会处理收件邮箱地址与发送所必需的信息。
  5. 兑换地区判定：为执行福利兑换的地区规则，系统会在你提交兑换时，根据连接来源判断所在的国家或地区；该判定仅用于决定兑换是否可用，不影响积分累计。

四、设备标识哈希

设备标识哈希由设备安装标识与硬件特征组合计算而成。服务器只保存哈希值，不保存可直接识别设备的原始硬件特征。

设备标识哈希仅用于：验证身份与保障账号安全；防范批量注册、刷取积分、伪造计时等作弊与滥用行为；核算访客每日额度；执行注册限制、产分限制与设备封禁；支持安全中心的设备展示与远程下线。

设备标识哈希不用于广告投放、行为追踪或用户画像，不会被出售或出租。

五、数据的使用目的

维护者仅在下列目的范围内使用第三节所列数据，不作无关使用：

  1. 创建与维持账号，验证身份，保障账号安全；
  2. 记录与结算积分，处理兑换、审核与发放；
  3. 提供好友与端到端加密聊天服务（仅中继密文与分发公钥包）；
  4. 处理意见反馈，运营论坛并实施内容审核与管理；
  5. 防范作弊与滥用，执行封禁、禁言等处置并处理申诉；
  6. 完成你主动发起的检查更新、订阅导入或更新、邮件发送等请求。

将数据用于上述以外的新目的时，维护者将依法另行告知，并在需要时取得你的同意。

六、数据的存储与服务商

账号数据存储于维护者运营的账号服务器，当前采用 Cloudflare 提供的云服务（Workers 与 D1 数据库）。邮件经邮件发送服务（当前为 Resend）发送。

存储地点或主要服务商发生重大变更的，维护者将通过修订本说明、协议修订或官方公告告知。

七、保存期限与删除

  1. 本地数据保存在你的设备中，直至你自行删除、清空或卸载处理（见第三节第（一）款）。
  2. 账号数据在账号存续且提供服务所必需的期间内保存。你在安全中心申请注销后进入 7 天冷静期，冷静期内登录即可撤销；冷静期届满后，维护者将删除你的服务器账号数据，包括通行密钥公钥凭证与加密聊天密钥备份，删除后不可恢复；同时账号积分清零，未使用的兑换码作废。
  3. 法律法规要求留存的个别日志记录，不随注销立即删除，将在法定期限届满后删除。
  4. 反馈、论坛、审核与安全审计记录，在实现问题处理、内容管理、安全审计、申诉复核与争议处理目的所必需的期限内保存，目的实现后删除或匿名化处理；依法必须留存的除外。
  5. 第三方服务提供方处理的数据，其保存期限由其自身规则确定。

八、我们不做的事

  1. 本软件不集成任何第三方广告、统计或行为追踪组件。
  2. 维护者不出售、不出租你的账号数据，不将其用于与本软件无关的营销。
  3. 软件内仅有 QFClash 自身的介绍与宣传内容，出现在「关于」页等固定位置，不弹窗、不影响操作。

九、你的权利与行使方式

你可以通过安全中心查看与管理你的账号信息与登录设备，包括修改密码、更换绑定邮箱、查看并远程下线设备、申请注销账号；可以在积分页面查看余额与逐笔流水。

你有权依法向维护者提出查询、更正、删除账号数据的请求，或咨询数据处理事项。可通过安全中心自助办理，或通过第二节邮箱联系；维护者将在核实身份后处理。依法或因安全、争议处理需要暂不能满足的，维护者将说明原因。绑定邮箱无法使用时，可通过邮箱申请人工核验。

十、未成年人

未成年人请在监护人陪同下阅读本说明与《QFClash 用户使用协议》，并由监护人决定是否同意。监护人认为未成年人未经同意提供了数据的，可通过第二节邮箱联系维护者，核实后依法处理。

十一、本说明的更新

维护者可能根据功能、数据处理方式或法律法规要求更新本说明。更新将提高版本号，并通过应用内公告、官方网站公告或其他适当方式公布；涉及数据处理范围、目的或你的权利等重大变更的，将重新告知，并在依法需要取得同意时取得你的同意后实施。更新后的说明自其载明的生效日期起生效。

QFClash Privacy Notice

Version: 1.0 ｜ Effective date: from the release date of QFClash 1.0.0 ｜ Applies to: QFClash for Android, QFClash for Windows, and the official website and forum services that share the same account system

1. Introduction

This Privacy Notice explains what data QFClash processes in providing the software, account, and related services, why it is processed, how it is stored, and what rights you have and how to exercise them.

This Notice elaborates on and supplements Section 7 of the QFClash User Agreement. In the event of any inconsistency between this Notice and the QFClash User Agreement, the QFClash User Agreement shall prevail.

2. Operator and Contact

QFClash is operated by an individual maintainer. For privacy-related inquiries, account data queries, and rights requests, please contact:

  • Email: a718983610@gmail.com

Before handling a request involving account data, the maintainer will verify the requester's identity to prevent data from being claimed, altered, or deleted by the wrong person.

3. Data We Process

(1) Data stored only locally on your device

The following data is stored only on your device (in the app's private directory). The maintainer does not actively collect, upload, or sell it:

  1. Subscription links and subscription configurations;
  2. Node selections, traffic statistics, and speed-test records;
  3. Software settings;
  4. Diagnostic logs (received by the maintainer only when you actively export and send them, and used only to address the issue you reported).

You may clear configurations and statistics within the software. After uninstallation, data in the app's private directory is normally deleted along with the app; files you exported and saved elsewhere may continue to exist, so please check and delete any files you no longer need before uninstalling.

(2) Data processed on the server when you use account services

When you register, sign in, and use account features, the maintainer processes the following data on the server:

  1. Account details: your self-chosen account ID, email address, password processed with irreversible encryption, account status, and registration time.
  2. Profile: the nickname and avatar you set. Your nickname and avatar are publicly displayed in the software, the forum, and friend contexts; your account ID is visible only to your friends; your email address is not displayed to others and is visible only to you under "My".
  3. Sign-in and device information: the name of the signing-in device, platform type (Android / Windows), most recent activity time, sign-in region, and the device identifier hash (see Section 4 for its composition and purposes).
  4. Session and security records: records of sign-in token issuance and use, verification code sending and verification records (including verification records when viewing redemption codes), sign-in and security operation logs, and ban and appeal records.
  5. Points and redemption records: usage heartbeat records, points ledger entries (the time, reason, and balance of each change), and redemption orders and review records. When you redeem a USD reward, this also includes the payout information you provide: a USDT (TRC20) receiving address, or the receiving email you provide if you choose a gift card, together with payout status and payout remarks; this information is used only for review and payout, and is visible only to you and the maintainer.
  6. Content you submit: feedback content and any contact details you voluntarily provide (the system automatically attaches the app version and platform information, and account information when you are signed in, to help locate the issue); posts and replies you publish on the forum, and related moderation, report, and enforcement records.
  7. Friend and encrypted chat data: friend relationships and friend request records; chat encryption public key bundles (identity public key, signed pre-key, and one-time pre-keys); ciphertext of chat messages; and chat key backups encrypted with a key derived from your account password. Chat uses end-to-end encryption: the server stores and relays only ciphertext and public key material and cannot read message plaintext; the key material of encrypted backups is encrypted with a key derived from the password you set, and the maintainer cannot decrypt it.
  8. Passkey public key credentials: after you create a passkey, the server stores its public key credential, credential name, creation time, most recent use time, and signature counter. The private key of a passkey always remains on your device or in your system's passkey service and is never uploaded to the server.
  9. Google sign-in information: when you choose Google sign-in, the maintainer reads your email address within the scope of the information returned by Google, in order to create or match an account. Signing in with Google and registering with email using the same email address are treated as the same account.

Guest mode requires no account registration. To account for the daily guest allowance, the server processes the device identifier hash and records of the date and used duration relevant to allowance accounting.

(3) Connection information third parties may process when you connect

  1. Update checks: when you actively check for updates, the software sends requests to the official repository or related release services; those providers may process connection information such as your IP address, request time, and client version under their own applicable privacy rules.
  2. Subscription import or update: the software sends requests to the subscription address you specify; the relevant providers and network service providers may obtain the connection information needed for the request, and their processing is governed by their own rules, which the maintainer cannot control.
  3. Account requests: sign-in, registration, and verification requests are sent by the software directly to the account server and are not forwarded through proxy nodes.
  4. Email delivery: emails for registration, password recovery, email change, exceptional verification, and redemption payout notifications are sent through an email delivery service, which processes the recipient email address and the information necessary for delivery.
  5. Redemption region determination: to enforce the region rules for benefit redemptions, when you submit a redemption the system determines your country or region from the connection source; this determination is used only to decide whether redemption is available and does not affect points accumulation.

4. Device Identifier Hash

The device identifier hash is computed from a combination of the device installation identifier and hardware characteristics. The server stores only the hash value; it does not store the raw hardware characteristics that could directly identify a device.

The device identifier hash is used only to: verify identity and safeguard account security; prevent abuse such as bulk registration, points farming, and forged usage timing; account for the daily guest allowance; enforce registration limits, points-earning limits, and device bans; and support device display and remote sign-out in the Security Center.

The device identifier hash is not used for advertising, behavioral tracking, or user profiling, and will not be sold or rented.

5. Purposes of Use

The maintainer uses the data listed in Section 3 only for the following purposes, and not for unrelated uses:

  1. Creating and maintaining accounts, verifying identity, and safeguarding account security;
  2. Recording and settling points, and processing redemptions, reviews, and payouts;
  3. Providing friend and end-to-end encrypted chat services (relaying ciphertext and distributing public key bundles only);
  4. Handling feedback, operating the forum, and carrying out content moderation and management;
  5. Preventing cheating and abuse, enforcing bans and mutes, and handling appeals;
  6. Completing requests you initiate, such as update checks, subscription imports or updates, and email delivery.

If data is to be used for a new purpose beyond the above, the maintainer will inform you separately as required by law and obtain your consent where required.

6. Storage and Service Providers

Account data is stored on the account server operated by the maintainer, currently using cloud services provided by Cloudflare (Workers and the D1 database). Email is sent through an email delivery service (currently Resend).

If the storage location or a major service provider changes significantly, the maintainer will inform you by revising this Notice, revising the Agreement, or making an official announcement.

7. Retention and Deletion

  1. Local data remains on your device until you delete or clear it, or handle it by uninstalling (see Section 3(1)).
  2. Account data is retained while the account exists and for as long as necessary to provide the services. After you apply for account deletion in the Security Center, a 7-day cooling-off period begins, during which signing in cancels the deletion; after the cooling-off period expires, the maintainer will delete your server account data, including passkey public key credentials and encrypted chat key backups, and the deletion is irreversible; at the same time, account points are cleared and unused redemption codes become void.
  3. Individual log records that laws and regulations require to be retained are not deleted immediately upon account deletion and will be deleted after the statutory retention period expires.
  4. Feedback, forum, moderation, and security audit records are retained for the period necessary for issue handling, content management, security auditing, appeal review, and dispute handling, and are deleted or anonymized once those purposes are achieved, except where retention is legally required.
  5. Data processed by third-party service providers is retained according to their own rules.

8. What We Do Not Do

  1. The software integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking components.
  2. The maintainer does not sell or rent your account data, and does not use it for marketing unrelated to this software.
  3. The software contains only QFClash's own introductory and promotional content, appearing in fixed locations such as the "About" page, with no pop-ups and no interference with operation.

9. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

Through the Security Center you can view and manage your account information and signed-in devices, including changing your password, changing your bound email, viewing and remotely signing out devices, and applying for account deletion; on the points page you can view your balance and per-entry ledger.

You have the right, in accordance with law, to request that the maintainer provide access to, correct, or delete your account data, or to inquire about data processing matters. You may do so yourself through the Security Center, or contact the maintainer via the email in Section 2; the maintainer will handle your request after verifying your identity. Where a request cannot be satisfied for the time being due to legal requirements or security or dispute-handling needs, the maintainer will explain the reason. If your bound email is no longer usable, you may apply for manual verification by email.

10. Minors

Minors should read this Notice and the QFClash User Agreement in the company of a guardian, who shall decide whether to consent. If a guardian believes a minor has provided data without consent, the guardian may contact the maintainer via the email in Section 2, and the matter will be handled in accordance with law after verification.

11. Updates to This Notice

The maintainer may update this Notice in light of changes in features, data processing, or legal requirements. Updates will carry a higher version number and will be published through in-app announcements, announcements on the official website, or other appropriate means; for major changes involving the scope or purposes of data processing or your rights, you will be informed again, and where consent is legally required, the change will be implemented only after your consent is obtained. An updated Notice takes effect from the effective date stated in it.
