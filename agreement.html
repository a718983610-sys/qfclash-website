9. Points Rules

  1. How points are earned. While a signed-in user is using the Software with the connection in a valid state of use (the core is handling traffic), usage time accumulates by the minute; every full 3 hours earns 1 point, and time of less than 3 hours is kept as unsettled minutes and is not cleared. Points progress can be viewed on the points page. The Android and Windows versions share the same account's points; if two devices are used at the same time in the same period, the overlapping period is counted only once, while non-overlapping periods accumulate normally.
  2. Accounting basis. Points are accounted for by the server, based on the timing records and points ledger received by the server; the display on the client is for reference only; the balance can be recalculated from the ledger. Every user can view the balance, total earned, total spent, and per-entry details on the points page.
  3. Caps. Each account may earn at most 8 points per day; the points-earning cap per device per day is likewise 8 points; both caps apply at the same time, and once either is reached no further points are counted that day.
  4. Situations in which no points are counted. No points are counted for the following periods: periods of prolonged idling with no actual traffic (30 consecutive minutes with no traffic increment); periods for which timing records are missing, out of order, or abnormal in time; and periods of running in abnormal environments such as emulators, device spoofing, or multi-instance clones (the system will indicate "the current environment does not earn points", but normal use is not affected and no ban is imposed).
  5. Registration limits. To prevent bulk registration, at most 1 account may be successfully registered per device within 24 hours; exceeding the limit triggers a 72-hour cooldown, during which registration requests from that device will be refused; a device that reaches 3 registered accounts within 7 days will be flagged and reviewed.
  6. Cheating prohibited. You must not forge or tamper with timing records or device identifiers in any way, register accounts in bulk, rotate accounts on the same device to farm points, or exploit program defects to obtain points.
